# Shared legal pages

Edit product content in `tools/legal-content.cjs`, layout and footer links in
`tools/legal-template.cjs`, and presentation in `shared/legal.css`.
Run `node tools/build-legal.cjs`; use `--check` to detect stale generated output.
The studio and Calendar statements are distinct documents with separate scopes.
The shared policy footer is rendered in HTML, so links work without JavaScript.
`tools/chrome.js` also uses it for future generated studio pages.

## Release limitations

These documents are implementation-informed drafts, not a legal certification.
Before a public release, obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review, confirm the
operator identity/location and provider agreements, and complete an accessibility
audit including host-selected colors, keyboard booking and screen readers.
Resolve advertising-consent requirements for studio pages: existing studio tags
load without an opt-in gate. Calendar and the new policy pages do not load them.
Define and implement a retention/deletion process and verify support request
handling. Review older Hebrew/Russian studio statements and other generated pages
before updating those versions; they have not been regenerated by this change.
Do not advertise full compliance or a completed audit on the basis of these pages.

References reviewed:
- https://developers.google.com/workspace/workspace-api-user-data-developer-policy
- https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG-EM/
- https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/

Calendar footers reuse the studio privacy and accessibility links. Hebrew and
Russian links resolve to the existing /he/legal/ and /ru/legal/ pages through
shared/legal.js. Keep Calendar data disclosures under /calendar/legal/#privacy;
do not copy the studio translations into new Calendar pages.
