Privacy Policy

Red Crown Interactive, based in Haifa, Israel, operates this studio website. Contact: hello@redcrowninteractive.com.

Information and purposes

When you contact us, we receive the name, email, project details and message you provide. Providing information is voluntary, but we need contact details to respond. We use it to answer your inquiry and manage the resulting business relationship. Hosting and measurement services may process technical request data, such as IP address, browser, page and visit time. We do not sell your personal information or add you to marketing mailing lists without your consent.

Service providers and measurement

GitHub Pages hosts the studio site. Web3Forms processes contact-form submissions for delivery to us. Studio pages may use GoatCounter for visitor statistics and Google Ads for advertising measurement; the Google tag may use cookies. Browser privacy controls can restrict these technologies. Links to WhatsApp, LinkedIn and other external services open services with their own privacy practices. Providers may process information outside your country.

Retention, security and rights

We retain correspondence for the inquiry and resulting relationship, and where necessary for legal obligations or disputes. We use access controls and HTTPS, but cannot guarantee absolute security. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your information; applicable rights and exceptions depend on the law and circumstances. We may need to verify your identity. This site is intended for adults.

We aim to keep information accurate but do not guarantee uninterrupted access or error-free content. External services have their own terms. Nothing here excludes liability or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Israeli law applies, subject to any mandatory protections that apply to you. Contact us about disputes or questions.

Accessibility Statement

We aim to make the studio website usable with keyboards, assistive technology and browser zoom, guided by WCAG level AA and applicable Israeli accessibility requirements.

Status and limitations

A complete independent accessibility audit has not been completed. This statement is not a certification of conformance. Interactive 3D demonstrations and third-party services may present barriers. Please contact us for a text explanation or assistance accessing information.

Accessibility contact

Send the page address, the difficulty you encountered and, if helpful, your browser or assistive technology to hello@redcrowninteractive.com. We will investigate and work with you on an accessible alternative.

Support

Contact Red Crown Interactive at hello@redcrowninteractive.com for support, privacy requests or accessibility assistance. Include the affected page, what happened and your preferred way to receive a response. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, calendar tokens or private booking-management links.
