Accessibility Statement

Red Crown Interactive believes the web should be usable by everyone, and we invest in making the Site accessible to people with disabilities, in the spirit of the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities regulations and Israeli Standard 5568, which is based on WCAG guidelines at level AA.

What is implemented on the Site

  • Semantic HTML structure with a logical heading order, and text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Full keyboard operability with a visible focus indicator; interactive panels can be closed with the Escape key.
  • ARIA labels on interactive controls, including the 3D viewers, the language switcher, and the project cards.
  • Support for the "reduced motion" system preference: animations and moving effects are disabled for users who request it.
  • Color contrast designed for a dark theme, and text that scales with browser zoom.
  • The Site is responsive and usable on mobile, tablet, and desktop, and is tested automatically across devices and browsers.

Known limitations

The Site includes interactive 3D model viewers. These are experiential by nature and may be of limited use with a screen reader; each viewer has a text description, and no essential information is available only through a 3D viewer.
