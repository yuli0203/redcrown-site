END-TO-END PRODUCT DESIGN

Concept & UI/UX Design

We shape the concept, user journey and visual language together with the technical plan. For AR/VR, interface design includes the surrounding 3D space, how people reach and handle objects, and how guidance supports the task.

3D Asset Creation

We create high-end 3D models, animation and visual effects in the studio, integrating the artwork into the working product. The enzyme model and Robi instructor shown in our portfolio are examples of our in-house 3D work.

Immersive Experiences

Design and code work together in Unity: hand tracking, physics-based object interaction and controller input chosen for the experience. We balance visual clarity, comfort and the demands of the target device.

HOW A PROJECT RUNS

Focused module or pilot

One experiment, one procedure, one tool. Launches in weeks.

  1. 30 minutes Introductory call What you teach or build, what is not working, and whether this is even the right tool.
  2. 1 to 2 hours Scoping session We go through the material together and agree what the module has to do.
  3. Within days Written proposal Scope, milestones and a clear price, before any work begins.
  4. Weeks Build Development, 3D and interaction, with checkpoints you can steer from.
  5. Before handover QA on real devices Tested on the headsets and phones it will actually run on, not just in the editor.
  6. Ongoing Handover and support Installed where it runs, with the people who will use it shown how.

Larger system

A course, a classroom, or a platform. Built in milestones with a team.

  1. 30 minutes Introductory call The same starting point: what you need and whether we are the right studio for it.
  2. Half a day Scoping workshop With your teaching or engineering staff, so the requirements come from the people who will live with it.
  3. 1 to 2 weeks Technical plan Architecture, platform choices, milestones and budget, written down before commitment.
  4. Selected per project Team assembly Development, 3D and design specialists brought in and managed under the founder, who stays hands-on.
  5. Months Build in milestones Each milestone is something you can put in front of real users, not a status report.
  6. Every milestone QA on real devices Multi-device and multi-user testing, because a class of twelve fails differently from one headset.
  7. On your premises Deployment on site Hardware, installation and the instructor workflows around it.
  8. Ongoing Handover and support Training, documentation and updates as the system keeps evolving.
