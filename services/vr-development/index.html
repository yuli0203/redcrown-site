VR development for science and engineering

We build virtual reality software in Unity for universities, research groups, and engineering teams: guided experiments, training simulations, and multi-user classrooms that have to work the same way for every student, every week.

WHAT WE BUILD

Guided experiments

Hour-long procedures a student works through themselves, operating the equipment, making decisions, and reading real instrument values, with a virtual instructor setting the pace.

Multi-user classrooms

Up to 12 Meta Quest 3 headsets running in parallel, each paired with the instructor’s tablet, so a whole cohort goes through the same session together.

Training simulations

Procedures that are expensive, slow, or unsafe to rehearse on real equipment, rebuilt so they can be repeated as often as the trainee needs.

BUILT SO A WHOLE CLASS CAN WEAR IT

Comfort decides whether VR survives contact with a real timetable. If a handful of students feel unwell, the session stops being usable for teaching. Movement inside our experiences is deliberately designed to keep motion sickness to a minimum, so a full hour in the headset stays comfortable for an entire class.

The same thinking covers the parts nobody demos: headset logistics, charging, resetting between groups, and what an instructor does when one device misbehaves five minutes into a lesson.

PLATFORMS AND TOOLS

Built in Unity, primarily for Meta Quest 3, with hand tracking, gesture, and controller interaction chosen to fit the task rather than to show off. Where a project needs to reach beyond the headset we also build for desktop, WebGL, iOS, and Android.
