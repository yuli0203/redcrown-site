    USEFUL TO KNOW

    A few practical answers.

    Is Red Crown Calendar free?

    Red Crown Calendar is being built as a free scheduling tool by Red Crown Interactive. There are no paid plans or subscriptions in this preview.

    What can I use right now?

    The separate calendar preview lets you select dates and meeting lengths, save meetings in this browser and export them as .ics files. It does not send invitations or sync with external calendars.

    How does sign-in work?

    Create an account with Google or an email address and password. Email accounts include verification and password recovery. Microsoft sign-in is coming soon. Signing in does not grant access to your calendars, and preview meetings are currently saved on this device only.

    Can I choose any meeting length?

    You can choose a preset or enter a custom duration in minutes. The current preview supports 1 to 480 minutes, subject to the available working hours and breaks.

    Can I choose my colors and logo?

    Booking-page customization is planned: choose your colors and upload your logo to match your brand. These controls are not available in the preview yet.

    Who is behind the tool?

    Red Crown Interactive builds apps and interactive software. Red Crown Calendar is a free tool from the studio. Learn about Red Crown Interactive ↗
