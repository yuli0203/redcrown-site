USEFUL TO KNOW

A few practical answers.

Is Crown Calendar free?

Crown Calendar is being built as a free scheduling tool by Red Crown Interactive. There are no paid plans or subscriptions in this preview.

What can I use right now?

The separate calendar preview lets you select dates and meeting lengths, save meetings in this browser and export them as .ics files. It does not send invitations or sync with external calendars.

How will sign-in work?

Google and email/password sign-in are being set up. Account registration will open once the authentication service is connected. An account will not automatically grant access to your Google Calendar.

Can I choose any meeting length?

You can choose a preset or enter a custom duration in minutes. The current preview supports 1 to 480 minutes, subject to the available working hours and breaks.

Who is behind the tool?

Red Crown Interactive builds apps and interactive software. Crown Calendar is a free tool from the studio. Learn about Red Crown Interactive ↗
